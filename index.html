About WSMS – Water Supply Management Software in Pakistan

WSMS (Water Supply Management System) is a Business Management Solution Developed by Orion Fx to help Mineral Water Suppliers & Distributors across Pakistan manage their daily operations more efficiently. From Customer Accounts & Water Canes Delivery Records to reusable 19-litre Canes Inventory, Filling Records, Invoicing, Payments & Expense tracking, WSMS brings essential business activities together in one organized system.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make Water Supply Business Management simpler and more efficient by providing practical digital tools for Pakistani Suppliers & Distributors. WSMS helps businesses organize customer relationships, track deliveries and reusable Water Canes, Maintain Financial Records & reduce losses caused by inaccurate can deposits or incomplete transaction records.

Our Vision

Our vision is to support the digital growth of Pakistan's Mineral Water Distribution industry by making effective Business Management Software accessible to Suppliers & Distributors of different sizes. Through continuous improvement, user-friendly features & reliable operational tools, WSMS aims to help Water Supply Businesses improve accountability, manage resources & deliver better customer service.
