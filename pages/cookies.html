Cookie Policy

Understanding how we use cookies and local storage in WSMS

Last Updated: January 2025

This Cookie Policy explains how WSMS uses cookies in compliance with Pakistan's electronic transaction and privacy standards.

SECTION 1. ABOUT COOKIES

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser.

WSMS uses cookies only for essential functionality.

SECTION 2. TYPES OF COOKIES WE USE

WSMS uses only two categories of cookies.

2.1 Essential Cookies

Used to:

  • Keep you logged in
  • Maintain session state
  • Load your local data correctly
  • Make navigation smoother

Without these cookies, WSMS will not function properly.

2.2 No Tracking Cookies

WSMS does not use:

  • Advertising cookies
  • Analytics cookies
  • Third party cookies
  • Behaviour tracking
  • Marketing trackers

The platform is offline and does not communicate with external networks.

SECTION 3. WHY WE USE COOKIES

Cookies are used to:

  • Identify active login
  • Maintain session authentication
  • Reduce repeated actions
  • Keep the app stable
  • Support offline operation

SECTION 4. HOW YOU CONTROL COOKIES

You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

If you do this, WSMS may stop working because essential cookies are required for:

  • Login functionality
  • Saving preferences
  • Loading local records

SECTION 5. DATA STORED BY COOKIES

Cookies may store:

  • A temporary session token
  • Basic app state
  • Device preference settings

Cookies do not store:

  • Customer data
  • Delivery records
  • Financial information
  • Personal details
  • Tracking identifiers

SECTION 6. COOKIE SECURITY

Cookies used by WSMS are protected by your browser.

They are not shared with any third party because the app runs offline.

SECTION 7. DELETING COOKIES

You can delete cookies at any time through your browser.

Deleting cookies may remove your session and may reset your experience.

SECTION 8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

WSMS may update the Cookie Policy. The latest update date will appear at the top.

SECTION 9. CONTACT

If you have questions about cookies, contact us through the information provided on the website.

End of Cookie Policy.
