Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how we protect and manage your data

Last Updated: January 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how WSMS handles your information. WSMS follows the laws of Pakistan, including Article 14 of the Constitution regarding privacy, the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act 2016 and principles of the Draft Personal Data Protection Bill.

SECTION 1. NATURE OF WSMS

WSMS is an offline tool.

It does not send your data to the cloud.

It does not store anything on a server.

All information stays inside your browser on your device.

SECTION 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

WSMS collects only the information you enter. This includes:

  • Your name
  • Your company details
  • Customer information
  • Delivery records
  • Cans delivered, received and filled
  • Expense entries
  • Payment details
  • Station information
  • Inventory records

WSMS does not collect sensitive data such as CNIC numbers, bank information, biometric data or location tracking.

SECTION 3. TECHNICAL INFORMATION

WSMS may use:

  • Browser type
  • Session status
  • Basic system errors for performance improvement

WSMS does not use analytics trackers, third party scripts or advertising cookies.

SECTION 4. HOW YOUR INFORMATION IS USED

Your information is used to:

  • Run WSMS features
  • Save your business records
  • Generate calculations and charts
  • Maintain login session
  • Improve performance and fix errors

SECTION 5. DATA STORAGE

Your records are stored in browser localStorage.

This means:

  • You control your data
  • Clearing browser storage deletes everything
  • Using a different device resets your account
  • WSMS cannot restore deleted data
  • WSMS cannot see or access your information

No external databases or servers are used.

SECTION 6. DATA SECURITY

Your data is protected by browser security.

You are responsible for:

  • Securing your device
  • Using a strong password
  • Avoiding public computers
  • Keeping your browser updated

No system is fully secure. You accept the risk of storing data on your device.

SECTION 7. DATA RETENTION

WSMS keeps your data as long as it remains in your browser. You can delete it at any time by clearing browser storage.

SECTION 8. DATA SHARING

WSMS does not share, sell or trade your information.

Data is shared only when required by:

  • A lawful order by a Pakistani court
  • A legal request under PECA 2016
  • Your explicit consent when you export data manually

SECTION 9. YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to:

  • View your data
  • Correct entries
  • Delete everything
  • Stop using WSMS
  • Export your records manually
  • Refuse to provide any information not required for operation

SECTION 10. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

WSMS is not for users under 18 years.

SECTION 11. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

WSMS does not transfer any information outside Pakistan.

You control any exports manually.

SECTION 12. JURISDICTION

All privacy related matters fall under the courts of Karachi, Sindh.

SECTION 13. POLICY CHANGES

WSMS may update this policy. The updated date will appear at the top.

SECTION 14. CONTACT

For privacy questions, contact us using the information provided on the website.

End of Privacy Policy.
